## **Research Interest**
My research interests lie at the intersection of construction engineering and advanced technologies such as deep learning and computer vision. I focus on developing AI-based systems to enhance safety and efficiency on construction sites. This includes creating deep learning algorithms for real-time hazard detection and multi-camera object tracking, as well as frameworks for low-light image enhancement to ensure reliable performance in diverse lighting conditions. Additionally, I am dedicated to smart construction and infrastructure monitoring, utilizing UAV images, CCTV data, and weather information for early issue detection and structural health monitoring. My work in Visual Question Answering (VQA) aims to streamline hazard identification and reporting, thereby improving operational efficiency and safety compliance. Furthermore, I explore AI-based disaster management systems, particularly for forest-fire response, leveraging deep learning for early detection and damage assessment. Through domain adaptation and transfer learning, I ensure the robustness and applicability of AI models across various construction environments, ultimately advancing smart construction practices and contributing to safer, more sustainable infrastructure management.
