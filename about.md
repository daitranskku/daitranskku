    <h2>Background</h2>
    <p>I am Dai Quoc Tran, a Postdoctoral Researcher specializing in Construction Engineering and Management. I completed my Ph.D. at Sungkyunkwan University in Korea, where I developed expertise in applying deep learning-based computer vision to improve safety in construction sites.</p>
