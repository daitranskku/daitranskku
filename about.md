# About Me

I am Dai Quoc Tran, a Postdoctoral Researcher specializing in Construction Engineering and Management. I completed my Ph.D. at Sungkyunkwan University in Korea. My research focuses on using deep learning-based computer vision to improve safety in construction sites. I am also interested in the application of deep learning in non-destructive testing and evaluation (NDT/NDE) and structural health monitoring (SHM).
