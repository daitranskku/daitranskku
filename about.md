## **Academic Background**

I completed my **Ph.D. in Construction Engineering and Management** at [Sungkyunkwan University](https://www.skku.edu/) in Korea, where I developed expertise in applying deep learning and computer vision techniques to construction safety challenges. My research focuses on creating intelligent systems that can automatically detect hazards, monitor worker safety, and optimize construction processes through AI-powered analytics.

---

## **Research Philosophy**

My approach combines **theoretical rigor** with **practical applications**, ensuring that research outcomes translate into real-world solutions for the construction industry. I believe in the power of interdisciplinary collaboration, bringing together expertise from computer science, civil engineering, and artificial intelligence to address complex challenges in construction safety and automation.
